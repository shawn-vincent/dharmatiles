Settled (9 Nov 2026): the T is 6 deep, as the template maker has it (OpenSCAD's 7 lies inside the pocket, so the cut is the same). The pocket is OpenSCAD's: open through the bottom, 6.0 to 10.7 in, not two slots at the face. Its roof is 6.1, half a millimetre over the socket (neither reference had that), and the base is 7.0. What dharmatiles does not copy: the open face between sockets and a socket at every 12.7 mm (our tiles keep a solid wall and the grid-line subset, which meets the official places), and the roof ramp.

The clip (OpenLOCK_Clip_v5.4.stl): one 3.5 mm slab, z 1.75 to 5.25, 19.5 along and 13.2 across, centred on the tile face. A stem 3.7 deep (1.85 each side) × 13.2 wide sits in the 14 mm mouth (0.4 clear each side, 0.15 in depth). Each half is 9.8 wide from 1.85 to 5.5 in, swells to 10.66 at 6.5 in (the snap bulge) and ends at 9.75 in with 45° corners. Against our socket: 0.35 mm clear above and below the slot, 0.85 under the pocket roof, 0.95 short of the pocket's back wall, and the bulge touches the T's last millimetre by at most 0.12 mm per side (0.01 mm² of the clip), which is the detent. The official 2×2 gives the same 0.01 mm².

Checked by slicing our 1×1, 2×1 and 2×2 OpenLOCK builds the same way: at z 0.3 the 1×1's pockets match the official I template to 0.03 mm (area 561.19 against 561.18 mm²), and on the 2×2 the T half-widths at depths 2.1, 3, 4, 4.9, 5.5 and 5.9 mm (5.97, 5.67, 5.32, 5.02, 5.0, 5.0) and the void heights (1.4 to 5.6 in the T, 0 to 6.1 over the pocket) equal the official E.
