Reference sources

Outside work we measure our geometry against. Read them for dimensions and behaviour; do not copy or port their code into the program. Re-download with the commands below.

Finding (8 Nov 2026): our two "retention slots" are OpenSCAD's pocket read on the wrong axes: its cube([4.7,18,…]) sits at x = −8.35 after the profile is rotated, which is 8.35 mm into the tile, 18 mm along the edge. Ours puts 4.7 mm slots 8.35 mm either side of the mouth, opening the face, so at socket height a 25.4 mm square's face is open over about 21 mm instead of 14. Slicing STLs/OSOL-E-Imperial-8.0-None-var0.stl confirms the reference's shape. The T profile itself matches. The two references disagree on the T's depth (7 vs 6) and on the pocket; the official template settles both.
