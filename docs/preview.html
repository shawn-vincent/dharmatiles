dharmatiles preview

Preview

A live, painted 3D view of your tiles in the browser. It rebuilds a tile every time you save it, paints overhang the way the printability gate sees it, and shows a mistake at its line. It is part of the program and works offline.

Run it

dharmatiles                                   # your tiles and the library, in the browser
dharmatiles preview my-tile.dtile --open      # one document
dharmatiles preview a.dtile b.dtile walls/    # several files and folders
dharmatiles preview lib:tiles/cliffs          # one library group
dharmatiles preview my-tile.dtile --system ol # for OpenLOCK
dharmatiles preview slicer-export.stl         # any binary STL, through the same gate

With nothing named, the list has two sources: My tiles ($DT_HOME, else ~/dharmatiles) and the Library. Folders are watched: a new tile appears in the list within a second, and a deleted one leaves it.

It serves http://127.0.0.1:7878/ (the first free port from 7878, or --port) on the loopback address only, and refuses a request whose Host is not 127.0.0.1, localhost or [::1]. Ctrl-C stops it.

What it shows

  • The tile on the plate, painted like the PNG renders, with its squares ruled on the plate.
  • Overhang from the gate itself: faces between 55° and 65° are orange, faces past 65° red. The colours come from the gate's own flags, so the view and build cannot disagree.
  • The record: whether the tile passes the gate and why not, its stack (each entry linked to its element page) and the audit's numbers.
  • Progress while a tile builds: the stage, an element's own count (grass blades, wall stones, tree leaves) and the time left, estimated from the last build of the same tile.

Moving round a tile

Orbit with the left button, pan with the right button or Shift, zoom with the wheel or a pinch, toward the point under the pointer. A fling keeps turning and settles. Picking a view turns the tile there rather than cutting.

KeyDoes
1–9Views: iso, top, front, right, back, left, low, close, under
pPerspective or orthographic, keeping the view
cPaint on or off (off is plain grey plastic; overhang colours stay)
oOverhang colours
wWireframe
gThe grid on the plate
tLight or dark

Choices are remembered in the browser. With reduced motion set in your system, views cut and nothing coasts.

Live updates

Save the document, or any file it uses, and the tile rebuilds. The old tile and your camera stay until the new one is ready. A mistake shows over the view as file:line:column with a hint, in front of the last good build; the next good save clears it. One tile builds at a time: tiles named on the command line are built at the start, and a tile picked from a folder is built when you pick it.

Paint

Every material is painted like a quick acrylic job: a dark basecoat, mid tones, and pale highlights drybrushed on whatever stands proud, so joints and cracks stay dark and edges catch the light. Each material's ramp is a line in library/palettes/default.dtile. Give your own copy to dharmatiles preview --palette my.dtile, which repaints when you save it, or to build --png --palette my.dtile.

Limits

  • Editing in the view is designed but not built: see the editor.
  • Made for your own machine: the mesh is sent whole (about 90 bytes a triangle), fine on the loopback, not meant for a network.
  • A change rebuilds the whole tile.
  • No section cuts or measuring.
  • --system is fixed for a run; restart for the other base.
