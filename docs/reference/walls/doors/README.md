Geometric truths (all references agree):

1. **A doorway = opening + SURROUND + optional leaf.**  The surround
   is structurally distinct masonry: dressed jamb blocks (quoin-like
   alternating stacks) + an arch of voussoirs or a straight lintel.
2. **The wall's own bond flows AROUND the surround** — courses butt
   into the jamb stones; nothing is "cut out" of blocks.
3. **Voussoirs are radial**: wedge blocks perpendicular to the arc,
   springing from the jamb tops; keystone at the apex.  Fieldstone
   arches use thin SLABS radiating (ref-03/05); dressed arches use
   larger wedges, sometimes in 2–3 concentric orders (ref-05).
4. **Door leaves are planked rectangles** (vertical planks, battens,
   studs, ring/handle), square-headed even under an arch (tympanum
   fills the difference, ref-02) or arched-top matching the surround
   (ref-01, official UD-010).
5. Sills/thresholds: a flat slab at the base of the opening.
