Notes from the collection

Recurring shape vocabulary (for the rework)

  1. Rounded but not spherical — egg/loaf/hump masses, one axis longer, broad flattish or gently domed top.
  2. Sit low and heavy, seated into the ground — widest near the base, often partly sunk; ground meets rock in a buried contact line, not a clean tangent.
  3. Big soft facets + rounded arrises — a few large planar-ish faces meeting at rounded edges (roundover weathering is right; keep facets few and large).
  4. Two-tone / blotchy surface — lichen-pale or moss crown, darker shaded/damp lower face. (For later, when colour/texture is in scope.)
