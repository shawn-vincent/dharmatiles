• A print list's plates are planned in the browser editor, with no program: opening a print list or a map in the editor in a web page lays its pieces out on the printer's bed (the pieces' tiles built in the page, the plate in view in full), shows its scene, its crowns and its plates' panel, and changing the plate in view or a word of the plates line (the printer, the mode, the grouping, the gap, the margin, turns) is answered there as by the program. Planning is dt-service's plates_calls, written once on a host the program (its build lanes and disk cache) and the editor's worker (its own turn, the page's clock) both are. Taking a word off the plates line (the page sends it as an empty value) now does take it off: the program's own handler left the word on the line.
