• Print lists and plates: a .dmap document lists tiles to print (piece "lib:tiles/ground/1x1-grass" count=8 seed=12 system="ol") and, on a plates line, the printer, the print mode (single colour or multicolour), the grouping, the gap, the margin and turns; it is read with spans and mistakes at their text, and listed in folders. dharmatiles plates my-list.dmap builds every distinct piece in full (from the cache when there is one), packs them onto the printer's bed large to small by each piece's real footprint (a tree's crown over the edge included; a 256 mm bed holds 6 × 6 squares, a 220 mm bed 5 × 5) keeping clear of the printer's excluded zones and never needing more filaments on a plate than the printer has, and writes one Bambu Studio project with every plate (objects and instances placed on their plates, paint per triangle in multicolour, none in single colour, the settings of your template; round-tripped through Bambu Studio: plates, instances, positions and paint survive), or one STL (or plain 3MF) per plate for another printer. The printer's filament slots are checked before anything builds, and a multicolour grouping that costs a plate says so and offers packing by size only. Only a full, gate-checked build is exported; a low or super-low build never is. See Plates.
