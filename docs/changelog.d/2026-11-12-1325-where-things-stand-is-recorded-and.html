• Where things stand is recorded, and the tiers agree on it: every build carries where its rocks, trees, flowers and grass clearings were placed (Built::placed, before any detail is drawn), kept in the disk cache with the build, and the cache has three tiers of its own, full, low and super-low (the editor’s draft is a fourth, never kept by the program), so the three of one tile are three entries. A build option, cells, sets the lattice by hand. Measured on the library: rocks and flowers stand at the same coordinates at 32, 48 and 128 cells, but a tree’s site is picked from a list of lattice nodes and moves 3 to 10 mm with the lattice, and a cliff’s scree changes its count; making those lattice-free would move the full builds’ trees and so their hashes, so the lattice is not what tiers vary: a low build keeps the full build’s lattice and a test checks that it places everything where the full build does.
