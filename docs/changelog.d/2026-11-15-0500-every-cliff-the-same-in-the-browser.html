• Every cliff tile is the same in the browser as in the program: two causes of a few triangles' difference are gone. glamx’s symmetric 3x3 eigenvalues (which order a convex hull’s first mesh) now use libm, not the platform’s acos and cos; and Manifold’s DedupeEdges splits its duplicate edges in one order (sorted) in series and in parallel, where the series code used ring-visit order and so numbered the new vertices otherwise (a carried patch, applied to the browser’s Manifold as well).
