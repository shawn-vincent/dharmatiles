• One tier, one verdict, one system: a build's detail is one Tier (full, draft, low or super-low) in its options, so no illegal pair of flags can be written; whether a build passed the printability gate is decided in one place, and every STL, 3MF, plate and Bambu writer takes the proof of a pass, so a low or failing build never reaches a file (a tile that fails the gate still writes its record and its views, and plates wait until every piece passes); the connector system is one System (db or ol) parsed where a word comes in; and the engine's "no seed" is null, not -1.
