The reader of format 0's values is gone: its unitless dialects, its (mm) and (h) annotations, and its "x,y" point reader, which nothing but its own tests still read. A parameter default with an annotation is refused like any other value. A height formula with a unit in it now gets its span when its parent(…) is flawed; the old reader could not read it and skipped it.
