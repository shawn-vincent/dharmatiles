• Cards that cannot hurt a phone: a card’s Full detail button says what the build will need (about 2 GB and 20 s for a grass tile, an estimate the engine makes from the tile and holds to measurements) and turns itself off, pointing to the desktop program, on a device that cannot carry it; the result cache is bounded by bytes, a full build is never kept and its worker is replaced so the heap shrinks; a draft builds through its card’s step cache, so editing grass does not carve the cliff again; the engine’s files carry their hashes and are kept for the next visit, and the page’s own examples are kept between visits; on a touch screen a finger scrolls the page and Turn it turns the tile; a failed engine start no longer strands the waiting cards, and a runaway build is stopped at three times its estimate.
