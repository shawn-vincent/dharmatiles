• A scatter cover falls evenly and lies against what stands: pattern=cover places its things at an evenly spreading sequence of points instead of random darts, so no patch heaps up and none is left bare (random darts made piles with bare ground between). Under a cover gap is 0 unless written, so things touch a rock, wall or trunk without going into it, which lets a near fringe stand at the foot; a spaced scatter is unchanged.
