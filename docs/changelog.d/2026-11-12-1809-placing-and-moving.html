• Placing and moving: a catalog drawer along the bottom of the map (the folder level with each tile’s real picture, its size and have or need; press a card to put the tile down, drag it to choose where), dragging a piece or the selection in a view looking down (snapping to the squares of its own system, a place that is taken becoming the nearest free one), R to turn, D to duplicate, ⌫ to remove, New map in a folder you own; every change is an edit of the map’s text with Undo (the toast, or ⌘Z) and the view shows it at once.
