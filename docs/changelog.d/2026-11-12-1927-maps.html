• Maps: a .dmap piece with at="(2sq, 1sq)", rot (0, 90, 180, 270 clockwise), bottom (a $ name or a length: the height of the tile’s zero above the map’s ground, 0 by default), seed and an id (piece p3 "lib:tiles/walls/1x1-wall-stone" …) is placed on a grid of squares of its own system; the map may say grid cols=8 rows=5 and let $plinth "20dmm", and a print list is a map whose pieces have no at. The title bar has a Map beside Tile and Plates: a document with placed pieces opens as a map, the program reads its scene at once (each piece’s tile, seed, place, turn and bottom), and the 3D view draws every piece’s mesh placed, turned and raised with Iso, Top, Low and Close as presets. The panel’s levels are the map, one piece and a property; Open tile goes to the tile with the map one click back. See Maps.
