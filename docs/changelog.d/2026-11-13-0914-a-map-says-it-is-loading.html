• A map says it is loading, the way a tile says it builds: the pieces of a map being built are one job of its document, told as the document’s progress and as a running message (Loading the pieces of first-plate), with the pieces done of the pieces asked for, the piece being built and an estimate once one is done. The status line (loading 30% · 3 of 10 pieces · 1x1-grass), its bar and the messages panel’s running row all read that one record, the same one a tile’s build is, and the map’s own count of N loading is gone. A job ends cleanly when its last piece is done, withdrawn or superseded, and a piece that cannot be built is an error in the panel.
