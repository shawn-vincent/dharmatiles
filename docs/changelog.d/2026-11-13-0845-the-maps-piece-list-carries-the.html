• The map's piece list carries the shelf: the pills no longer float over every piece (a switch in the … menu, off until turned on, still shows them); the panel lists the pieces instead, in Placed, Not placed and Left off, a row per tile with its count, have, to print and other colour, with a sort (Name, Count, To print first), a filter (All, To print, Have, Other colour) and a search over the names, kept for each map. Pointing at a row outlines its pieces in the view, pressing it selects them, and a selected piece's row is marked and scrolled to.
