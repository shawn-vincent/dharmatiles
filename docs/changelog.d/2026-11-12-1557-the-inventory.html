• The inventory: inventory.dinv in My tiles is a log of what you have printed, a KDL v2 document with a line for each print (print "lib:tiles/ground/1x1-grass" seed=97 system=db colour=multi result=ok on="2026-11-09" hash="3c1f9a4e" from="crossing/plate-1"), loss (gone) and rename (renamed), read with spans and mistakes at their text; counts are worked out from it and never stored. A piece is the same piece when its tile, seed, system and colour mode are the same (DungeonBlocks and OpenLOCK never count for each other; two prints are two; a failed print is kept and does not count; edited since printing is information only). dharmatiles inventory [list | have | gone | relink] reads and adds to it; nothing is made until the first line is written. A print list's piece takes print=missing (the default), always or skip, the plates line takes inventory=check|ignore, and dharmatiles plates prints only what the inventory lacks (--all prints every count, and a list that is all on your shelf says so).
