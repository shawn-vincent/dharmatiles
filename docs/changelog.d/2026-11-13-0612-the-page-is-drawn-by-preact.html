• The page is drawn by Preact, one panel at a time: Preact, htm and @preact/signals are vendored beside three.js (no build step, nothing fetched at run time). The page’s facts are signals (every document’s state, each key of what the program says and of the view), the panels read them, and a panel is drawn again by itself when one it read changes, so the hand-written redraw and its signature string are gone. Every panel is drawn this way now: the map, the plates, the printer chooser, the places and folders, the inventory and the tile’s property sheet with its levels, crumbs and strip. They look the same and keep their slides and scrubbing; a seed or length you are half way through typing, the printer search, a spacing field, a reprint form or the sheet after a print are no longer lost when the file changes under them, and after a scrub and an Undo the sheet no longer fails to come back. The mode of a map (Map or Plates) is worked out from the document and your choice, not stored. Every call of the page goes through one request layer: of the checks, names, offers and drafts asked in a row only the newest one’s answer is taken, whatever order they come back in, and a write is never dropped. Which file the Source shows is the id of the document it holds, not a second variable.
