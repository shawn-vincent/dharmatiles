• Every command is timed: each cargo xtask command, and each step of check, prints its time against its budget, logs it to dt-times.tsv, and prints an OVER BUDGET banner when past it. The check builds as a step of its own, held to the warm build’s budget (or the cold build’s in a fresh checkout), so its test step times only the tests, and it ends with every step’s time on one screen. The budgets are one table, xtask/src/budget.rs.
