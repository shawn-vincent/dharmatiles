• A param passed on into a thing param’s entry is typed by the control it fills: stand s=5, where s ends up as piece size=s on a pebble, is now refused with dt::unit at the call’s argument, as pebble size=5 is, and the same for a wrong kind, a range and a word, through any number of definitions that pass s on; a param’s default is typed the same way. A param that fills two controls of different kinds (a length and a share) is dt::type at the param, saying both. No tile changes.
