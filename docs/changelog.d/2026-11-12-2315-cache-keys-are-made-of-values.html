• Cache keys are made of values, not of printed text: the in-memory step cache and the disk cache both key on one canonical form of what a build reads (every float by its bits, every text and list with its length, nothing of where a value was written), and the disk cache digests it with the sha2 crate in place of its own SHA-256. The disk cache’s format number is 2, so entries kept by an older program are ignored (they are cleared as they age out) and nothing is misread; builds and hashes are unchanged. Weighted ranges and seeds live in a small crate of their own, dt-value, which the geometry kernel no longer holds.
