• The map crossfades a change of resolution: when a piece's mesh changes (a box becomes a tile, super-low becomes low, low becomes full, or back) the old mesh stays drawn under the new for about 0.3 s and each pixel goes to one of them by an 8 × 8 Bayer pattern in screen space (the new one keeps the pixels under a threshold that rises from 0 to 1, the old one the rest), so the two share every pixel exactly: no holes, no transparency to sort, depth right, and the paint and overhang red and orange are each mesh's own throughout. A swap that comes while one runs goes on from the blend it has, a piece that leaves the map mid-fade goes at once, an old mesh is freed once nothing draws it, nothing draws while no fade runs, and with reduce motion the swap is at once. The rules for the layers are pure (web/preview/js/viewer/crossfade.js) and tested.
