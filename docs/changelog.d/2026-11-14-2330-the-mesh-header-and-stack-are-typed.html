• The mesh header and the stack are typed end to end: the JSON that goes with every mesh (its kind, box, face counts, stack, clouds, debug marks, and a plate's bed) is one type, MeshHeader, made by one constructor and written by serializing it, in place of six hand-built forms and a key rewrite. A stack entry is one type, StackItem, in the check's answer, the plan and the messages' entry lookup, and the tile's shown is a Shown record. The page's types for all three are generated from them into api.d.ts, and the files that read them are now checked by tsc.
