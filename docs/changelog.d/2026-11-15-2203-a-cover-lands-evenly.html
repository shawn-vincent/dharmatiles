• A scatter cover lands evenly against what stands: under pattern=cover a thing keeps clear of a rock (or, for dry things, a rock, tree or wall) by its real outline, the footprint near measures from, not a disc round its bounds, so the cover touches the rock on every side and leaves no halo on a narrow rock's short side. A place that falls where nothing could stand is thrown again at the next point of the even sequence, and one that falls within reach of the outline is moved straight out to lie against it, so the count holds and the gaps do not cluster. Spaced and clumped scatters are unchanged.
