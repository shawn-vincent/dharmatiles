• The 3D view owns its camera: the page tells the view what it shows (the tile, map or plates, in which view, which preset was asked for, the map’s box and the piece Close frames) and the view decides where its camera goes from that alone, with one memory of the last view you left each document in each of its views. The five places that used to move the camera (the build’s following, the opening of a tile, the map’s mount and unmount, the preset buttons and the reframing on a resize) are gone; a test fails if a part of the page outside the view moves it. A map opens framed on its pieces, a preset frames again when pieces arrive, a tile being built widens to take in each part and settles on the finished one, a view you moved stays where you put it, and going back to a document restores its view, as before; a map now opens on the preset you last chose (it always opened on Iso).
