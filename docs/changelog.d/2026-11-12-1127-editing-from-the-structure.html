• Editing from the structure: a stack row has a ⋯ menu (Rename…, Hide, Delete, Move up, Move down, Add above…, Add below…; also F2, Delete, ⌥↑, ⌥↓ and the menu key on a row), the stack ends in Add to the stack and the Shape in Add line and Add region. Delete and Hide give an Undo line, not a question. Add lists every element, those the plan lets stand at that place first and the others with the plan's reason (POST /api/tiles/ID/offers: a default instance of each element planned at the place), writes the entry on a line of its own and opens its level. A stack row can be dragged by the mouse (a line shows where it will stand; Esc cancels): the new move edit (POST /api/edit {op: move, at, before}, dt_doc::edit::move_node) carries the node whole with the comment lines above it and its children, never doubles a blank line, and leaves a hidden entry where it is. A refused order is let through and the check says why in its own words, with an Undo. A move never writes an id=; the line over the view says when it swapped the looks of two same-named entries, and Rename… writes the id= that pins one (it draws that entry's look once, then keeps it). A scatter written inside a dirt moves with it, and an entry a definition call makes (call in the stack's items) is moved, hidden and deleted as the call. insert takes place: before with at.
