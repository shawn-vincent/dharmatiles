• Printer profiles by brand and model: a printer profile is also a machine. Beside its floors (line, layer, overhang, steepest, gap, which keep working as they were and which a machine may leave to the default profile) it can hold brand, model, bed, height, excluded zones, nozzle, slots (filaments), a slicer template, a verify mark and a from profile to start from. The library has 41 of them under library/printers/<brand>/ (Bambu Lab, Prusa, Creality, Elegoo, Anycubic, Snapmaker, Sovol and Voron, with bambu/x1c and bambu/x1c-ams and sovol/sv06 among them), the rows whose sources disagree marked verify in the file; --printer bambu/x1c builds for one (the output folder is out/db-bambu-x1c/, and a build is byte for byte what the default makes), your own go in printers/ beside your tiles, and Printers lists them. A mistake in a machine is shown at its text.
