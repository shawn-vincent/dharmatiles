• Draft, step and the reading calls are the service's: a draft and a step (an edit and the draft of what it made) are one function each in dt_service::draft_calls, written on a draft-lane seam of TileHost (the program queues the draft ahead of its builds and waits; a web worker builds it in its own turn). The calls that read a text without building it (names, colours, a typed value, what a length prints, the Add menu's offers) and the ones the page makes at startup (about, the element schema, the language's words, a document's text, the first snapshot) are dt_service::read_calls and elements_json, so the worker answers them by verb as the program answers them over HTTP. A refusal now carries a bug's help and backtrace and the “a newer one took its place” mark, and a worker tells the page a refusal as the same error body HTTP sends. Nothing changes on screen.
