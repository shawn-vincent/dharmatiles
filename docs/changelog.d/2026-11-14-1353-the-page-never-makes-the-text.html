• The page never makes the text of a tile, not even while a handle is dragged or a value scrubbed: each step of a drag or a scrub is one request (POST /api/tiles/ID/step) that makes the edit and drafts what it made, and answers the new text, its revision and the draft’s mesh; one request is in the air at a time and the newest value wins, so a fast drag skips steps instead of queueing them, and the whole change ends as one ordinary edit and one step of history. A map point is moved by Op::SetPoint (a line’s through point, a region’s fill point, a thing’s at=): the page says where the pointer is in squares and the program writes the point, and a line’s point on or near the outline lands on the nearest seam of that square’s edge, as its word, or on the corner (SeamTable::snap), so a drag cannot make a dt::seam mistake. A region’s boundedBy lines and an except’s regions are written by Op::SetArgs. The byte-splicing path (replaceBytes) is gone, and a test fails when a module splices text, puts text in the pane, or gives edited anything but a program answer.
