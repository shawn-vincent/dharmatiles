• Scatter patterns: a scatter says how its places fall with pattern: spaced (the default, as before), clumped (clumps to a square, each reaching spread) or cover (a dense cover of thousands a square, with no spacing checked among the things). cover (a share) and clearings (bare round patches, clearingWidth wide) mean what they mean on grass and work with every pattern. A near block makes the things answer to what the stack built before the scatter (of= rock, wall, tree, cliff, standing or water): within a reach drawn for each thing they stand taller (height, rocks and flowers) or are left out (thin). It is the engine side of a grass field being a scatter; every existing tile builds as before.
