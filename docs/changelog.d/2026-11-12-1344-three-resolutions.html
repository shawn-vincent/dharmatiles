• Three resolutions: full is the finished build; low (about 15 000 to 35 000 triangles, 0.1 to 1.6 s, against 0.2 to 1.7 million and 1 to 5 s) is built on purpose with the same places drawn with less detail: rocks as hulls, grass as a carpet, trees as smooth branches and rounded clumps, walls as sharp blocks, cliffs carved coarse, the ground meshed within a third of a millimetre, no union and no gate (faces flagged by angle, overhang still painted); super-low (at most 12 000) is cut from it. A low build says not yet checked, is never printed or exported (build --tier low --png writes views only and refuses --3mf), and dt_eval::build_tier and GET /api/tiles/ID/tier serve a tier by name. The tile editor keeps full builds and the draft while dragging; a stored low preview shows only while a tile builds and nothing earlier of it is kept, marked low · not yet checked.
