• A selected piece looks selected on the map: the rest of the map fades behind the selection, the way the rest of a tile does, with the one machinery: the map’s pieces take the tile’s fade state (applyFade), so the amount (FADE, with the hover’s HOVER_REST and HOVER_LIFT) and the tween (FADE_IN, FADE_OUT, HOVER_S) are the same code, not a copy; each piece is told its part in it (selected, under the pointer, the rest) and is drawn through the same two passes. Clearing the selection brings everything back with the same tween; the outline stays. Pointing at a row of the piece list lifts all its pieces.
