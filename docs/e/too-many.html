dt::too-many

The stack expands to more entries than a tile may hold.

What to do

Scatter fewer things, or call a definition fewer times.

The rule

The stack expands to more than 10 000 entries once every definition is called and every scatter is counted. Scatter fewer things, or call a definition fewer times.
