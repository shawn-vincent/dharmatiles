A parameter's kind is not a kind a parameter holds, or nothing says what it holds.

What to do

Write param name kind=thing, kind=layer, kind=length, kind=angle or kind=plain, or give it a default.

Broken, then fixed

The rule

A param's kind is what it holds: a length, an angle, a plain number, a thing or a layer. Its default, the control it fills or how the definition uses it usually say so, and then kind= is optional. Write it when nothing else does, or to say it plainly: param ground kind=layer, param leaf kind=thing, param reach kind=length. The words are the kinds' own (kinds). When it is written, the default, every use and every caller's argument are checked against it, and a disagreement is dt::type at the value, in the words of every kind mistake.
