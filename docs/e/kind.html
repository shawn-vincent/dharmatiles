What to do

Write param name kind=thing, kind=layer, kind=length, kind=angle or kind=plain, or give it a default.

Broken, then fixed

The rule

A param is a name of a kind: a length, an angle, a plain number, a thing or a layer. The kind is settled once, at the param, and never from where the param is used. kind= says it: param ground kind=layer, param leaf kind=thing, param reach kind=length. With no kind=, it is the kind of its default: param n 3 is a plain number, param w "5dmm" a length, param leaf pebble a thing. The words are the kinds' own (kinds).
