Error code · a note: nothing is refused

dt::crown

The crown of one piece reaches over a taller neighbour.

What to do

Move one of them, or raise the crown's piece; nothing is refused.

The rule

A tree's crown reaches past its square. When a piece's crown reaches over a neighbour that stands taller than the crown's lowest point, the stream carries a caution note, The crown of 1x1-grass-tree (p2) reaches over 1x1-wall-stone (p3), which stands 2 mm up into it, found from the pieces' real shapes (their draft builds, kept in the cache), not from their squares. It is a note, never a refusal: nothing is forbidden, the real shapes just meet there.
