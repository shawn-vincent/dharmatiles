Error code · a note: nothing is refused

dt::steep

Pins force the dirt past its slope.

What to do

Nothing is refused; move the pins apart, or soften their heights, for a gentler hill.

The rule

Pins force dirt past its slope: the dirt is forced to 43.8°, steeper than its 24° slope: 5.33 mm up to 5.59 mm over 0.27 mm. A note does not stop the build; check prints it after the tile’s line.

Read it in context: Heights · Pins that force dirt past its slope are noted
