Error code · a mistake in the document

dt::percent

The control is a share written as a percent, such as 20%.

What to do

Write the share with a percent sign, from 0% to 100%.

The rule

buried is a share written as a percent, buried="20%..50%" (weighted forms too), from 0% to 100%; % is a plain fraction, so buried=0.3 is the same 30% and buried=30 is out of range; a length there is dt::type.
