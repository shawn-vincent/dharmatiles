dt::region

A region cannot be made: it names a place that is not there, or leads back to itself.

What to do

A region is made of lines, fill points, within and except of other regions, none of which may lead back to it.

The rule

Every frame is a region, and regions nest. A point or a line says where a thing goes; the thing's size makes its region: a tree's crown round its point, a rock's disc, a wall's strip along its line, a scatter's area. A $region is the region of the thing being built (at a declaration, the region the declaration sits in), $tile is the root, and parent($region) is the region containing it.
