dt::base

base takes no settings yet.

What to do

Write just base: the connector is the build target's.

The rule

The stack is written bottom up and is exactly what is printed. base is the first line, or the tile is refused (dt::base-order); the land follows in stack order, things and grass where they are listed, and water where it is listed, over the ground above it. Heights are lengths measured from zero, the base's top, on which the first layer rests.
