dt::cloud

A top with odds (a cloud) is used in arithmetic it does not have.

What to do

Use a weighted range only where the guide's clouds section allows it.

The rule

A top may be a cloud: a low surface, a high surface and one weighting that says how often each height between them comes up. Write a weighted range in the top: top="$groundHeight + low(0dmm .. 3dmm)" is dirt between the ground height and 3dmm over it, mostly low with a few rises. A plain range, [a .. b], is a cloud with every height equally likely, and builds exactly as it always did.
