Element · base

openlock

The OpenLOCK base, by Printable Scenery: a 1-inch (25.4 mm) grid of tiles joined side by side with separate clips that slide into T-shaped slots in the base walls, one slot on each grid line between squares along an edge (one at the middle of a one-square edge).

Building for it

dharmatiles build my-tile.dtile --system ol
dharmatiles build --all --system ol

Every size written in dmm or sq scales with the square, like a photocopy: a 7dmm wall (a fifth of a square) prints 5.08 mm thick. The printability floors (one line, one layer) never shrink.

The pocket is where the clip's head sits. It is open to the table from the bottom of the base, so the roof over it, 0.9 mm thick, is bridged. The printer's bridge limit holds it with no support inside the slot. Where a pocket comes within about 8 mm of a corner (every pocket of a 1×1, and the short sides of a 2×1) its ends are cut at 45° and a 3 mm strut stands between it and the next side's pocket.

A lotus logo is inset 0.4 mm into the bottom. It sits at the middle of the tile, as large as fits without touching a pocket (22 mm at most) and is left out where that is under 6 mm: a 1×1 or a 2×1, where the pockets leave only a few millimetres at the middle.
