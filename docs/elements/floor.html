Element · surface

floor

A stone pavement that replaces the ground in its area: a wall laid on its side, one slab per square by default, some missing, spalled or cracked. Walls stand on it.

Writing it

tile size="1x1" seed=72 {
    stack {
        dirt
        floor texture=chipped seed=22
        wall along="north" style=cut-stone seed=23   // stands on the slabs
    }
}

A wall on a floor seats on the pavement; its top stays at the wall-top level.

Rules

What this element always does. A change to one of these is a change to the element.

  • A floor replaces the ground: no soil on top of it.
  • Its slabs carry a texture made for drybrushing.
  • Floors and walls share one stone engine.

Notes

The slabs share the wall's stone finishes; how they are made is on the wall page.
