Element · surface

ground

The bare ground shape: a smooth surface at a level, flat unless you give it bumps. Use it under water (the pool bed), or when one ground shape must run under several surfaces. For textured earth, use dirt, which shapes the ground too.

Writing it

ground                                  // flat, at the ground level (5 mm)
ground in=pool level="water - 2.2"      // a pool bed 2.2 mm under the water

On its own, ground makes a plain flat tile. In a divided tile it is usually the bed under water. A scatter written inside it lands on it.

Notes

Ground only rises from its level, never dips below it, except at the tile edge, where every surface meets the edge level (3.4 mm) so neighbouring tiles butt with no step. bumps up=3 down=1 sets rise and fall separately.

See dirt for how the ground surface is textured, and levels for the named heights.
