Try this

  1. Click a rock in the pool. Click it again to climb to its scatter, the pool, the tile.
  2. Drag a value in the card beside it, sideways. Watch the text.
  3. Drag the rock size past 12 mm: the gate fails and the undersides go red. Click the verdict.
  4. Type rock in the bar and click the bank. Then drag the new rock.
  5. Type put three rocks on the bank, or why is it red.
  6. Type seeds. Long-press a number in the card to sweep it.
  7. Click a line in the document, or mute an entry in the stack.
  8. ⌘Z undoes anything. Narrow the window to see tablet and phone.
