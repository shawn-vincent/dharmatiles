Units

UnitIs
sqa square of the grid; 35 mm on DungeonBlocks, 25.4 mm on OpenLOCK
mma printed millimetre: the same on every system
ina printed inch, 25.4 mm: the same on every system
dmmdesign millimetre: 1/35 of a square; 1 mm on DungeonBlocks, 0.73 mm on OpenLOCK
layerone printed layer: the printer's layer height
dega degree
rada radian: 180/pi degrees
pithe number pi: 2 pi rad is a turn
%a plain fraction: 50% is 0.5 (a length only inside a point)

Seam words

Along a square’s edgenorthsouthwesteast
1/3 (a third)nnwsswwnwene
1/2 (half)nswe
2/3 (two thirds)nnessewswese

The tile’s corners: nw (north-west), ne (north-east), se (south-east), sw (south-west). They take no number.

Read it in Lines.
