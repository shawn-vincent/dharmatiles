Pipeline

  1. Ground: grasslab's rises-only hills (the field), plus a bank across the tile.
    • Shoreline position x_s(y) = 33 mm + 11 mm·n₁₄(y) + 5 mm·n₅.₅(y), giving bays and headlands plus coves. Each n is smooth noise normalised to 2σ and clipped, so a single extreme peak can't flatten the rest. Land is on the left.
    • For signed distance s = x_s − x: G = bed + (hills − bed)·smoothstep((s
      • 3)/8 mm), with bed = 0.4 mm.
    • The waterline (G = 2.6 mm) is where the bank crosses the water level, so its shape follows the hills as well as the wobble.
  2. Rocks: rocklab scatter (6–8), posed. Each rock is classified as wet (centre in water) or dry. All footprints block grass.
  3. Soil: meadow free lenses on land, plus a mud strip.
    • The strip covers land within w of the waterline, where w is smooth noise mapped to 0–4 mm. It is zero over part of the shore, so there grass reaches the water.
    • Water (G < WL) is a grass blocker: no rooting, and blades stop 0.5 mm in, lapping over the waterline.
  4. Grass flow: grasslab flow, wrapped around lenses and rock circles (meadow), plus shore steering.
    • Within 9 mm of the waterline, headings that point away from the water are compressed toward along-shore, and others lean toward the water. It is applied as a smoothed rotation (same as steer_rim).
  5. Grass: grasslab.build_grass(blocker = water ∪ rock footprints): grasslab's stroke blades on dirt (its own defaults; the shore no longer overrides the grass).
  6. Water: waterlab.Scene.from_fields(G sampled on the water grid, wet rocks), then gen_swell_chaos.
    • Water surface z_w = WL + relief·rim·smoothstep(d/0.4) + tide.
    • Water solid: a heightfield of z_w where z_w > G inside the basin (the water mask dilated by 0.6 mm), otherwise G − 0.3 (buried). Without the basin limit, the tide (±0.6 mm) flooded low soil lenses on land into puddles (v0a).
  7. Rocks: seated with meadowlab's seat_rocks. Against the water surface the grass top is taken as WL + 0.6 mm, so wet rocks emerge from the water.
  8. Reeds: 3–5 clumps in the shallows (G within −1.1…+0.2 mm of WL), built before the water so their stems can emit ripple rings, Poisson-placed at least 12 mm apart.
    • Each clump has 5–12 blades with a lemon (pointed-oval) cross-section: base half-width 1.3–1.8 mm and half-thickness 0.35–0.45 of that. They are extruded and tapered moderately (scale 0.25–0.35 at the top) to a blunt point, height 8–13 mm.
    • They rise from a common base (within 0.6 mm of the clump centre) and curve outward: each blade is warped by k·z², so it rises vertical and bends out to a 14–34° tip angle. The flat face points toward the centre, plus 8° toward the water.
    • Chunky at the base so they don't snap. Cattails were dropped in v0e at Shawn's request: "nothing more sophisticated". The contract stays at the base: the blade is about 1.8–2.6 mm wide.
  9. Union: grass ∪ water ∪ rocks ∪ reeds ∪ slab ∪ DB base, then stl_safe.
  10. Check: a painted preview with grass, mud, water, rock and reed colours, plus oblique, top and low views, written as a timestamped contact sheet.

v0k Bambu: "floating regions" on shore s1 A slicer-style island check (0.2 mm layers, shapely) found 15 islands of 0.002–0.08 mm² at the carved grass ledges. Plain grasslab and dirtlab have none. Reeds: half-width 1.3–1.8, height 8–13 mm, bases within 0.6 mm of the clump centre, fanning 10–32° outward (evenly spaced around the clump, flat face toward the centre) plus 8° toward the water, tip scale 0.25–0.35. Overhang audit 0.25 mm²; top 14.3 mm, so the peg stays short.
v0e Shawn: reeds should be blades (diamond or lemon section, tapering to a point), short and chunky lemon-section tapered blades; cattails removed
v0d Shawn: reeds in water should have small ripples circling out from them reeds root in the shallows (ground −1.1…+0.2 mm about WL, mostly wet). Each stem footprint becomes waterlab's sc.reed_mask, and gen_swell_chaos adds tiny rings: band 1.8–3 mm, 0.45 mm amplitude, e^(−d/4.5 mm). They are added rather than blended because reeds sit at the shore, where the land's dominant waves would drown them in a blend.

waterlab gained Scene.from_fields (scene from an external ground plus obstacle solids) for this lab. Its ENCLOSE_FREE went 0.3 → 0.4, so a single bank stays exactly un-enclosed.

Exposed ground = dirt (2026-10-29)

Shawn: dirt should be the texture wherever ground is exposed, across all labs, and mud and other dirt textures are eliminated. This lab now uses dirtlab.dirt_relief(seed, W, H): dirtlab's soil roughness, clods, grit and pebbles, without its hills or big stones, as a relief offset sampled onto this lab's grid.

  • The mud strip now carries the dirt texture through grasslab; it is still a strip of bare earth by the water, just not a separate "mud" texture.

  • Reed tips (2026-10-29): Shawn wanted tips sharp, not flat, but fairly blunt, blending smoothly into the blade.

    • Each blade is one continuous taper (a warp_batch of a 48-division extrusion). Its scale falls linearly from 1 to the tip scale up the body, then follows an ogive s·√(1 − u²) over the last 2.2 × the top half-width, closing to a point (minimum scale 0.03).
    • There is no seam and no flat top, and the overall height is unchanged.
    • The first try, a hull cone on a flat top, left a visible crease.

  • Undercut audit (2026-10-29). Shawn spotted tips standing on vertical walls. Cross-sections showed two things:

    • neighbouring blades' skirts stand as walls under visible tips;
    • many carved gaps were sealed internal voids, because a covering blade buried them: 22 holes across 16 sections on s3. Half the "undercut" area (139 mm² of downward faces) was hidden inside the solid.

    New grasslab OPEN_AIR_MM (shore sets 0.6): air is morphologically opened with a 0.6 mm ball, only air connected to the sky is kept, and the rest is filled. The result on s3 is 4 holes and 68 mm² of genuinely visible undercut, with 0 slicer islands. A wider neighbour cut-back beside tips (TIP_MARGIN) was tried and reverted: it enlarged the voids and left post-like remnants.

Stroke blades (2026-10-29)

The shore was where the grass was reworked, blade by blade (grasslab/blades_demo.py; grasslab DESIGN "v3: stroke blades"). The outcome replaced grasslab's grass everywhere, so the shore-only GRASS_OVERRIDES (TIP_CARVE, SIDE_CARVE_ALL, gaps, OPEN_AIR_MM, SLICER_LAYER_MM, DROOP, RISE_EXP, blade sizes) are gone: those options no longer exist, and the shore blade sizes are grasslab's defaults. The v0h–v0k carve, gap, pocket and island fixes above are history. s3 now builds in ≈ 40 s, 0.95M faces, 48 MB; its top (15.01 mm) crossed the 15 mm peg threshold, so it takes the tall peg.
