Revision 3, 2026-10-28 (matches code v1e). Purpose (Shawn): "a tile with some rocks across it, and then grass and bare earth in amongst the rocks — see how these things interact." It combines grasslab/ and rocklab/, inherits all of their requirements, and defines only the interactions.

Requirements

  • REQ-M-1 [S] A run writes meadowlab/out/<cols>x<rows>-meadow-s<seed>.stl: one watertight DB tile with grasslab hills, grass and soil, and rocklab rocks. Deterministic per seed (byte-identical STL).
  • REQ-M-2 [R] Rocks are part of the ground. Each rock sinks below the soil everywhere on its footprint: it is seated at a low quantile of the ground under it and extended downward (a plinth of its own section) to just below the lowest ground, so on slopes the uphill side is bedded and the downhill side shows no gap. No rock floats or perches (M1).
  • REQ-M-9 [R] Rocks read as rocks. Every rock's top stands clearly above the surrounding grass: at least max(1.5 mm, 0.45 × its height), lifting it by up to 1.5 mm if needed. No rock is swallowed by grass or buried in a slope (M5).
  • REQ-M-3 [R] Grass flows around rocks. The flow parts upstream of each rock and rejoins behind it, as it does around a bare patch (grasslab REQ-FL-4). Grass never runs into a rock head-on (M4).
  • REQ-M-4 [R] Most rocks have no margin. Grass grows right up to the stone and presses against it. Only a minority of rocks (about 35%) have bare earth beside them, and then on one side (a wake downstream or a scour to one side), never a ring (M2). (Shawn, v1b: "having each rock in a big bare area is kind of weird.")
  • REQ-M-5 [R] Rocks are obstacles, not soil.
    • No grass roots under a rock's real footprint (its own outline where it meets the ground).
    • Fronds heading into a rock stop pressed against its face, tucked 0.5 mm in, and are lowered like any cut-short frond.
    • Grass beside a rock is lower (half height at the face, full height 1.2 mm out), so it laps against the rock's foot rather than heaping up over it.
    • Rocks don't trigger the soil-bank behaviours (spill, side ledges); those belong to bare earth.
  • REQ-M-6 [S] Bare-earth target. BARE_EARTH_FRAC (20%) is the fraction of the tile that is bare earth including ground under rocks. It is met by adding natural-size bare patches, never by scaling patch sizes (M3). (Shawn, v1d: "stop that scaling thing; let the 20% be underneath rocks too.")
  • REQ-M-7 [S] A handful of rocks. 6–9 placements asked for on a 2 × 2 tile. Grass stays dominant.
  • REQ-M-8 [S] The grass (grasslab REQ-B-*) and rock rules still hold, and the audit reports them.

Failure modes

# Failure Looks like
M1 Pasted rocks rocks sitting on top of grass, or floating on slopes
M2 Moat bare ground around every rock, or a uniform ring (v1a/b)
M3 Bald field aprons or one scaled-up patch merging into large bare areas (v1a: 32% soil; v1c scaling)
M4 Ploughing grass running straight into rock faces
M5 Swallowed rock a rock showing only as holes or a sliver in the grass, or buried on its uphill side (v1d seed 3)
