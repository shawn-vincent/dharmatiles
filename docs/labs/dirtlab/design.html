1. Core idea: one height field, built in scale order

Everything except the few larger stones is one height field z(x, y) on a 0.15 mm grid (467 × 467 for 2 × 2). A height field cannot overhang, so the earth and the small/medium pebbles are supportless by construction (REQ-FDM-1). Each scale is a separate stage, and stages combine by soft max (polynomial smooth max, width k), not by summing: a lump on the ground is a new surface that wins where it is higher, with a fillet of width k where it meets. Summed bumps give porridge (D1); soft-maxed shapes keep their identity and get soil-like creases.

ground  = EDGE + lift·w_tex + undulation + w_tex·soil_mid
soil    = smax(ground, clods)              (§2.2)
z       = soil + w_tex·grit  [+ cracks]    (§2.3, §2.6)
z      += stone soil banks; stones seated  (§2.5)
z       = smax(z, pebbles) + foot mounds   (§2.4)
z       = max(z, EDGE); z(rim) = EDGE      (rises only)
mesh    = uniform heightfield solid ∪ 1 mm slab box ∪ stones ∪ DB base

w_tex ramps 0 → 1 between 0.4 and 2.0 mm from the edge, so the outermost 0.4 mm is exactly at EDGE_MM (REQ-H-1).

2. Stages

2.1 Ground (ground_undulation)

Two Gaussian-filtered white noises (σ 10 mm + 0.35 × σ 4 mm). As grasslab make_ground: subtract the 35% quantile, soft-floor at 0 (plains), scale the peak to U(0.9, 1.6) mm, fade with a cosine ramp over 6 mm from the rim, cap the slope at 14°. A low fBm (2.2 mm, ±0.07 mm) adds broad soil roughness so tamped ground isn't glassy.

2.2 Clods (clod_layer)

  • Patchiness: a σ 6 mm noise field → density 0…1 (smoothstep). Candidate clods are rejected with probability 1 − density: cloddy patches and tamped patches (REQ-E-3).
  • Placement: Poisson(0.7/mm² × area) candidates, largest first, accepted when ≥ 0.6 × (r₁ + r₂) from placed ones (a spatial hash). Radius 0.35–1.5 mm skewed small (U^2.2). Mixed sizes plus partial overlap avoid a packed cell pattern (D3).
  • Shape (crumbs, not blisters, REQ-E-2): 1–3 lobes (main + satellites at 45–80% size, 0.4–0.9 r away), each an ellipse (aspect 1–1.7) with angular radius harmonics k = 2, 3, 5 (σ 0.22/√k) and profile h·(1 − ρ^2.6)^0.8 (flattish top, soft shoulder). Height 0.12–0.3 r, ≤ 0.45 mm; the whole clod is tilted (σ 0.05 mm/mm) and starts U(0, 0.5)·h below the ground (partly sunk). Lobes and clods soft-max with k = 0.06 mm.

2.3 Grit (grit)

dharmatiles.stone.noise.fbm, 2 octaves at 0.45 mm, ±0.045 mm, plus 0.12/mm² sand grains (radius 0.15–0.3 mm, 0.05–0.12 mm high, max-combined).

2.4 Pebbles (place_pebbles, pebble_shape)

  • Clusters: 4–7 Gaussian blobs (σ 3–7 mm) on a 0.25 floor, × (1 + 0.8 × lowness of the 3 mm-blurred ground), normalised; darts are accepted with that probability (REQ-P-3).
  • Counts and sizes: 5–9 medium (2–4 mm) and 45–75 small (0.5–1.5 mm) per square, skewed small (U^1.8); medium placed first; 60 darts each, ≥ 0.25 mm gap; no pebble inside a stone's footprint.
  • Shape: a flattened cap hp·√(1 − ρ^p), p ∈ U(2, 3.5) (2 = ellipsoid, higher = slab-like); height 0.25–0.45 D, footprint aspect 1–1.9.
    • Faceted (75%): the min of K tangent planes of the cap (6–11 medium, 4–7 small) at stratified random points, each tilted (σ 12°), lowered by 0.03–0.18 hp and capped at 65°. rocklab's one-facet-per-seed idea in 2.5D: crisp ridges, mixed facet sizes.
    • Worn (25%): the cap itself with K/3 shallow flats.
    • Polygonal skirt: 5–8 side planes at 58°, 0.82–1.05 a out, so the outline is angular, not a disc (D2), and no side is a vertical, grid-aliased wall.
  • Seating (REQ-P-4): base = the 0.5 mm-blurred surface at the centre minus U(0.25, 0.45)·hp; soft max into the soil with k = 0.08 mm (small) / 0.22 mm (medium), so soil fillets the foot. Medium pebbles first get a soil heap (0.2 hp Gaussian, 1.25 × radius). Field.pebble marks the cells where a stone won (used for paint).

2.5 Stones (make_stones, bed_stones)

2–4 rocklab rocks (rl.make_rock, D 4.5–7.5 mm skewed small), scaled to 0.65 in z (D5: rocklab's boulders read as upright teeth on dirt), random yaw, dart-thrown ≥ 2 mm from the rim and ≥ 4 mm apart. A 0.3 mm soil bank (Gaussian beyond 0.7 R) is added to z; the rock's base goes to min(lowest ground under 0.85 R − 0.25 mm, median − 0.4 h). The z-scale steepens downward facets, but they lie below the soil; measured overhang is 0.00 mm².

2.6 Cracks (crack_layer, --cracks, off by default)

5 random-walk polylines per 2 × 2 (0.3 mm steps, heading σ 0.35 rad, 20–60 steps), carved as a 0.35 mm-wide, 0.25 mm-deep rounded V (from a distance transform). They stay within the 0.3 mm lift.

2.7 Tile (assemble)

make_heightmap_solid(z, W, H, base_h=0, subsample=1) (a uniform mesh: waterlab found the adaptive mesher faceted smooth surfaces) ∪ an exact 1 mm slab box ∪ the stones (batch_boolean), then ∪ the DB base (make_base, peg from select_peg_height on the top). stl_safe (grasslab's export guarantee, copied: separate pinches, float32 + merge, keep the largest body, raise unless watertight).

2.8 Preview (painted, contact_sheet)

  • Paint per face: earth ramp (5 browns) by 0.7 × relief over a 0.8 mm blur + 0.3 × relief over 4 mm (what a drybrush catches); stones and pebbles grey by facing and relief; walls dark.
  • Views: painted, oblique (clay), top, low, close (20 mm, painted).
  • dharmatiles.render.render (pyrender). When it has no screen (a locked macOS session: pyglet sees zero screens), the sheet falls back to a matplotlib renderer of the finished tile's top surface (ray-cast from the mesh, so stones are included), Lambert-lit, surface only; captions then say "(fallback renderer)".

4. Iteration log

Round 2 (2026-10-29)

Shawn's feedback:

  • more relief;
  • no frame at the rim;
  • pebbles that register at tabletop distance;
  • stronger cloddy/smooth contrast;
  • texture visible everywhere at 0.2 mm layers. His slice of v1g showed large smooth plains.

Shared texture (2026-10-29)

dirt_relief(seed, W, H) returns a DirtTexture: soil roughness, clods, grit and pebbles on flat ground, as a ≥ 0 relief offset, 0 at the rim, on the 0.15 mm grid. It can be sampled onto any grid and is cached. It is used by grasslab (and so meadowlab and shorelab), waterlab and rocklab as the texture for exposed ground.
