Revision 1, 2026-10-29 (matches code v1g). Brief (Shawn): "Plain bare earth. No footprints, just dirt and pebbles. Maybe some cracks, but probably not even cracks, just texture and pebbles." A standalone experiment like grasslab/ and rocklab/: it may import dharmatiles primitives and rocklab read-only, never modifies src/dharmatiles/, and writes to dirtlab/out/.

1. What this is

One STL: a 2 × 2 DungeonBlocks tile (70 × 70 mm) of bare earth: dirt texture and pebbles. No footprints, grass, water or (by default) cracks.

1.1 Visual scales

Scale Size What it does
Undulation 4–10 mm wavelength, ≤ 1.6 mm rise Gentle ground swell; flat plains between.
Clods 0.35–1.5 mm radius, ≤ 0.45 mm high Rounded, soft-edged, irregular soil crumbs, in patches; tamped smoother ground between.
Grit ~0.45 mm, ±0.05 mm Fine roughness and sparse sand grains.
Pebbles 0.5–1.5 mm (many), 2–4 mm (some), 4.5–7.5 mm (very few) Faceted or worn stones, half-sunk, loosely clustered.

2. Requirements

2.1 Output and tile

  • REQ-OUT-1 [S] A run writes dirtlab/out/2x2-dirt-s<seed>.stl (-cracks suffix when cracks are on).
  • REQ-OUT-2 [S] One watertight body after an STL-style reload (float32, merge by position); the run raises otherwise. Ground, slab, stones and DB socket base (dharmatiles.bases.dungeonblocks) are fused.
  • REQ-OUT-3 [S] Deterministic per seed; build ≤ 10 s; STL ≤ 25 MB.
  • REQ-OUT-4 [S] --sheet --seeds … writes a timestamped contact sheet dirtlab/out/YYYY-MM-DD-HHMMSS-contact-sheet.png: a row per seed, views painted / oblique / top / low / close, a caption under every image.

2.2 Height

  • REQ-H-1 [S] The tile edge sits at exactly 4.0 mm above the datum (official DB dirt band) all round, with a 0.4 mm flat band, so adjacent tiles butt.
  • REQ-H-2 [S] Interior ground ≈ 4.3–5.9 mm (median ≈ 4.5 mm).
  • REQ-H-3 [S] Rises only: nothing inside dips below the edge level (grasslab REQ-HILL-8). Texture rides on a 0.3 mm lift so it never clips flat.

2.3 Earth

  • REQ-E-1 [R] Multi-scale. All four scales of §1.1 read at once.
  • REQ-E-2 [R] Clods are crumbs, not blisters. Each is 1–3 lumpy lobes with a flattish, tilted top and a soft foot, partly sunk.
  • REQ-E-3 [R] Patchy, not uniform. Clod density follows a ~6 mm patch field: cloddy areas and tamped areas, never even noise everywhere.
  • REQ-E-4 [S] No cracks by default. --cracks adds a few fine meandering cracks (0.25 mm deep), still above the edge level.

2.4 Pebbles

  • REQ-P-1 [R] Sizes. ~50–75 small (0.5–1.5 mm) and 5–9 medium (2–4 mm) per square; 2–4 larger stones (4.5–7.5 mm) on the tile.
  • REQ-P-2 [R] Stone-like. Faceted (crisp planar facets, polygonal outline) or worn (rounded cap with a few flats); flattish, elongated up to 1.9:1. Never round domes or discs (D2).
  • REQ-P-3 [R] Clustered loosely. 4–7 soft clusters plus a sparse background, biased into low ground. Never on a grid.
  • REQ-P-4 [S] Sunk. Pebbles bury 25–45% of their height and meet the soil through a soft fillet; medium ones get a soil heap at the foot. Larger stones sit ≥ 0.25 mm below the lowest ground under them, in a 0.3 mm soil bank. Nothing floats.

2.5 FDM (0.4 mm nozzle, supportless)

  • REQ-FDM-1 [S] No overhang past 45°. The earth and small/medium pebbles are one height field (cannot overhang); larger stones are rocklab rocks (convex, grounded), flattened and sunk. The audit reports overhang area (n_z < −sin 48°).

3. Failure modes (named)

# Failure Looks like
D1 Porridge uniform isotropic noise everywhere, no structure
D2 Bubbles round domes or discs, especially in clusters (v1, v3)
D3 Cells / bowls packed lumps forming a Voronoi-like cell pattern
D4 Rough concrete fine grit only, no clods or stones
D5 Teeth tall upright stones out of scale with the earth (v4)
D6 Pasted pebbles stones sitting on the surface, no bedding
D7 Freckles a regular sprinkle of identical dots (v7 grains)

4. Acceptance

  • S1: seed 1 reads as miniature bare earth with none of D1–D7.
  • S2: seeds 1–3 differ in clod patches, pebble clusters and stones.
  • S3: a print, primed and drybrushed: clod tops and pebble facets catch the highlight, the creases keep the base coat.

5. Out of scope

Footprints, tracks, puddles, grass, colour in the STL, integration as a dharmatiles layer.

Added 2026-10-29 (Shawn, after slicing)

  • REQ-D-TEX [S] Texture survives 0.2 mm layers everywhere. Within any 1.5 mm window, local relief (max − min) is at least 0.2 mm, which is one layer, over at least 97% of the interior. Measured by scratchpad/texcheck.py. v1g failed this on 33% of the tile: flat "tamped" areas printed as smooth plains.
