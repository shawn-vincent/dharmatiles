Revision 3, 2026-10-28 (code v1e). Code: meadowlab/meadowlab.py. It imports grasslab and rocklab as modules and passes its own knobs as arguments (no monkeypatching); all meadowlab knobs are its module constants.

Pipeline (main)

  1. Two RNG streams: grass (seed) and rocks (seed + 1000003), so rock changes don't reshuffle the grass and vice versa. Stream use is interleaved in a fixed order: ground, flow, swaths (grass) → scatter, pose (rocks) → free lenses (grass) → aprons (rocks) → flow wrap, grass (grass).
  2. Ground, flow, swaths: grasslab make_ground, make_direction, Swaths, unchanged.
  3. Rocks first: rocklab.scatter(…, ROCK_PLACEMENTS_2X2 = 6–9) then rocklab.pose, so each rock's yaw, and therefore its real footprint, is fixed before any grass is built.
  4. Footprint masks (footprint_masks, footprint_mask): each rock's slice 0.45 mm above its base (Manifold.slice), rasterised onto the 0.12 mm grass grid; kept per rock (for seating) and as their union.
  5. Soil:
    • Aprons (apron_shapes) for 35% of rocks: a lens with half-width U(0.5, 0.9)·R and stretch U(1.2, 1.9), axis along the flow, centred U(0.6, 1.0)·R from the rock toward a side drawn within ±90° of downstream 60% of the time (a wake), otherwise of upstream (a scour).
    • Free lenses: grasslab place_lenses with a large pool (8 per square, capped by the 80 candidates and 13 mm separation), ranked by grasslab's low-ground score. fill_bare_earth takes them best-first at natural size until (soil > 0.5) ∪ footprint ≥ 20%, measured on every 4th grass column (0.48 mm). The v1c size-scaling fit was removed: it let one patch grow dominant.
    • combine builds a grasslab.Lenses from the first k free lenses plus extra shapes, sharing the free lenses' border warp.
  6. Flow (rock_circles): finish_flow wraps the soil lenses plus a circle of radius R + 0.6 mm per rock (as a lens with equal half-length and half-width), so grass parts around rocks (REQ-M-3) with no soil involved.
  7. Grass: build_grass(…, blocker=footprint, tuck_mm=0.5, side_low=0.5, side_mm=1.2): no rooting inside a footprint; spines stop 0.5 mm inside it, with the usual k_h lowering. Frond height is ×0.5 at a rock's face, back to full 1.2 mm out, and falls to 0 within 0.8 mm inside the face, so grass laps against the rock's foot instead of swallowing it (each blade's height is scaled at its tip). The result's solid is the dirt ground ∪ blades.
  8. Seat rocks (seat_rocks, per rock):
    • Seat: z₀ = q₀.₃₅(H₀ over the footprint) − 0.3 mm. The v1d minimum buried the uphill side of rocks on slopes (meadow seed 3: one rock showed as two holes, another as a sliver).
    • Emerge: grass top = q₀.₇₅(T) over a 1.5 mm ring outside the footprint. If the rock top stands less than max(1.5 mm, 0.45·height) above it, the rock is raised by the shortfall, at most 1.5 mm.
    • Bed: a plinth (the rock's own section 0.15 mm above its base, extruded straight down) reaches 0.3 mm below the lowest ground under the footprint, so the downhill side never shows a gap (REQ-M-2). Where it shows, it reads as the rock's own vertical lower face.
    • The run reports each rock's top above the grass, its height, any lift and the deepest plinth.
  9. Union (attach_base): simplified grass ∪ rocks ∪ DB base (peg height from the tallest point), keep the largest body, report crumbs.

Differences from the parent labs (kept: changing them changes output)

  • The grass grid is always 0.12 mm (grasslab uses 0.1 mm at 1 × 1).
  • The grass is simplified before the union with rocks and base (grasslab simplifies after its base union).

Export guarantees (2026-10-27)

This uses grasslab's exact slab box and stl_safe; see grasslab DESIGN, "Export guarantees". Meadow seed 2 had a zero-volume fin from the rock/grass union, which stl_safe drops. Seeds 1–6 reload as one watertight body (re-verified at v1e).

Stroke blades (2026-10-29)

The grass is grasslab v3: separate stroke-built, smoothed blades on dirt (grasslab DESIGN "v3: stroke blades"). Seed 1 now: 2,905 blades, 1.58M faces, 79 MB, ≈ 85 s; bare earth 20.8% (15.7% visible); overhang audit 13.0 mm². The FDM-backstop figures in the measured table are from the retired height-field grass.
