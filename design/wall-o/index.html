How it is made

Mass

Three big boxes

The wall starts as three boxes side by side. Each has its own height, so the skyline steps down to the right, and its own front depth.

Courses

Level beds

Each mass is cut by horizontal beds, 8 to 13 mm apart. The courses of neighbouring masses do not line up.

Joints

Vertical joints, some running through

Joints split each course into blocks. A joint either repeats the x of the one below, so it runs through two or more courses, or keeps 3 mm clear of it.

Depth

Push each block back by its own amount

Each face moves back 0 to 5 mm. One block in the middle course of the left column goes back 6 to 8 mm and the block above it comes forward 2 to 3 mm, so a recess sits under an overhang. A few small blocks of the same rock are laid at the foot. Skyline blocks also differ in height. A shadow map from the key light turns every recess and joint dark without any extra geometry.

Cleave

Break faces along planes

Each block is cut by one or two planes tilted a few degrees off the face, so the face becomes two flat facets that meet at a sharp crack. Tops tilt slightly; sides crack the same way.

Chips

Bite corners and edges

Two to four planes per block cut off corners and edges, 1.2 to 3.4 mm deep, mostly on the top and front. Then a larger bevel: a chamfer plane 1.6 to 3.4 mm in along the top-front edge of most blocks, often another along a top side edge, a vertical edge or the bottom edge. The bevels are what make a block read chunky and broken, not cubic.

Hew

Fill each face with flat facets

Every face is first bent by two or three planes across it, each leaning 0.12 to 0.3, joined by min or max, so it has a few big flat facets and sharp cracks. It is then filled with jittered points about 3.5 mm apart and joined into triangles. The face boundary stays flat, so the block keeps square edges. The inside points are pushed in or out by up to 1.5 mm (0.8 on cut faces), and each facet gets its own tone, so crevices go dark and ridges catch the light. This is what makes a smooth plane read as hewn stone, and it is the generated wall above.

What is still off

Round 3 (v7): each face now breaks along two or three crack planes into large flat facets, the left return face shows beside the figure, and the tone is a dark cool blue-grey. Still off: the facets are still triangle-shaped where O's are irregular polygons with ragged edges; the skyline is too regular; the joints are shadowed gaps and not deep model cuts; no paint wear, no brown dirt in the joints, no ground rubble but the few loose blocks.
