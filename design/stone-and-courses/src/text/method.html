Appendix · How the photographs were measured

The reference panels (L, B, E, M, G, O, F, A, N and their crops) were measured against the {{figure_mm}} mm figure that stands in each (about {{figure_px}} px tall in a {{panel_px}} px panel); distances farther from the camera than the figure read short. Every measurement carries about ±{{tolerance_pct}}%. Row heights, stone widths, joint widths and facet sizes in each step are read this way and set that step’s defaults.
