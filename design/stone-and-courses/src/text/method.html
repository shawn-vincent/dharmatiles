Appendix · How the photographs were measured

The reference panels (L, B, E, M, G, O, F, A, N and their crops) were measured against the 28 mm figure that stands in each (about 295 px tall in a 800 px panel); distances farther from the camera than the figure read short. Every measurement carries about ±25%. Row heights, stone widths, joint widths and facet sizes in each step are read this way and set that step’s defaults.
