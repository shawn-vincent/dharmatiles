4 · Printing

A printer lays {{LINE}} mm lines in {{LAYER}} mm layers. A face that leans out more than {{OVER}} degrees is orange; more than {{STEEP}} is red. The gate allows a thousandth of the downward area in each band. OpenLOCK ({{SQUARE_OL}} mm) prints every length at {{OLF}} of its DungeonBlocks value, but the {{LINE}} mm line does not shrink.

Every printing rule is made by construction in one step, so most of what the gate would refuse is never built. What still fails is listed in Where the model breaks.
