7 · The plan

The Rust engine builds a cliff tile today as one dense mesh of grain: {{rust_tri_low_m}} to {{rust_tri_high_m}} million triangles, a 2×2 corner at {{rust_corner_gb}} GB resident (the library baseline of {{rust_baseline_date}}, in {{rust_baseline_source}}; not measured here). This model builds a 1×1 tile from {{stones_min}} to {{stones_max}} stones, each a few convex pieces. It goes into the engine in this order; each stage has a small torture tile and the check that gates it.

  1. Footprint and crest, then the row planes. Torture tile: a J corner with dip on. Check: planes never closer than a share of a row.
  2. The partition. Torture tile: an L footprint. Check: the cells cover all of course 0.
  3. Rise and span. Torture tile: preset T. Check: no cell reaches past its supports; no loose body.
  4. Prisms between planes, then joints. Torture tile: preset S. Check: every bed is exactly the top below it.
  5. The rock, then the graded outline and front. Torture tiles: forms L (lip) and E (ledges). Check: overhang budget.
  6. The union and the gate. Check: every shipped wall passes in every seed; today {{gate_apart}} of {{n_builds}} builds pass with bridges counted apart.
