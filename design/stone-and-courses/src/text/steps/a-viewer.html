Step 0 The viewer

The viewer shows what the build makes, one step at a time, and each control changes one word.

Goal

Show the build one step at a time, so that every word can be tried and its effect seen.

The editor at the top of the page is the algorithm made visible. The Level strip stops the build after any step; opening a panel moves it to the step that shows that panel. The icons on the canvas change what you look at: the model in 3D, the plan from above, the elevation from the front, or the costs. The grey, red and green dot at the top right is the printability gate (step 11).

Controls are inputs only. Everything the build reports is a view. Hover a control, or tap its label on a phone, to read what it does and jump to the step that explains it.
