Step 10 (L5) The foot band

A band of loose stones is scattered at the foot of the wall.

The cliff only says where the band is: along the first row, one band cell for every half unit of length, weighted by how high the crest is there, so tall parts shed more. The scatter is a separate entry of the scene, not part of the cliff: scree stones for each square are placed in the band, biggest first, each clear of the ones before it, reaching a short way out from the face.

Why. Loose stones sit on the ground, so they cannot overhang; the scene joins them to the ground, so the gate does not judge them.
