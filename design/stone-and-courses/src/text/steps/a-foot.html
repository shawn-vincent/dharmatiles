Step 10 (L5) The foot band

A band of loose stones is scattered at the foot of the wall.

Goal

Scatter the loose stones that lie at the foot of a real cliff, and nowhere else.

How

The cliff itself only says where the band is: for every half millimetre along the first row, one cell of the band, weighted from 0.5 to 1.5 by how high the crest is there (tall parts shed more).

The scatter is a separate entry of the scene, not part of the cliff. scree stones for each square are placed in the band: sizes 1 to 7 mm (never under 2.6 mm), biggest first, each clear of the ones before it, reaching up to 5 mm out from the face.

Takes in
the first row, the crest, scree
Hands on
a list of loose stones; not part of the cliff's union
Deep numbers: the scatter
  • a band cell for every 0.5 mm along the first row, weight 0.5 to 1.5 by crest height;
  • 40 tries per stone to find a place clear of the others (distance 0.8 of the sum of radii);
  • sizes 1 to 7 mm with bias 1.5, never under 2.6 mm; reach 5 mm from the face.

Printing

Loose stones sit on the ground, so they cannot overhang. They are joined to the ground by the scene, not by the cliff, so the gate does not judge them.
