Step 3a (L2) The face band

A course’s face stands between today’s face and one face depth behind it, drawn by the graded rule, on every free face that is switched on.

One rule draws the crest, a course’s face and a stone’s outline. Make a smooth wobble along the line, with bumps for how many wobbles; rank it; map each rank through the weighting’s quantile to a place t in the gap; a point’s distance is inner plus gap times t. The weighting says how far, bumps says how many, so the line has exactly the weighting’s mix of distances and only where they fall changes. Here the inner line is the footprint’s straight face, the gap is face depth, and each course draws its own line from its own keyed seed (a line for each course and each face). The cells stay convex; each stone is cut to its course’s edge when its cell is handed to the rock, and joints never move. Face depth 0 is the straight face, exactly.

Which faces. A free face is a side of the footprint that stands in the air: front, back, left or right. A seam (the wall going on into the next tile, or the hill behind it) is never free. The Free faces group in the Region panel has one switch each for front, back and sides, and they govern this band, recess (step 5a) and the lip together: front is on and the others off unless you turn them on. Each effect is written once, in the face’s own frame (along the face, and out of the wall), so the front is the plain case of the general rule and a corner where two faces are on is cut by both. Back acts only on a free-standing footprint (a wall’s back is a seam, or, with Wall on, the mirrored front); sides act on a free-standing footprint and on the step of an L, and the left and right sides each draw their own line from the one switch. The picture shows preset C with the front only, then with all three on.

stay behind sets a course never in front of the one under it: node by node the edge is the larger of the course’s own line and the one below, so the band itself adds no overhang (a head joint opened by a stone recessed in step 5a can). A lip course stands out on purpose and is left alone. With stay behind off, each course draws its own line and may overhang.

Why. The band gives a face variety without ledges; the running maximum is the price, so the face of a tall wall drifts toward the outer line. The foot band of step 10 is a different thing.
