Step 11 (L6) The union and the gate

The pieces are joined into one body, and the gate checks that it can print.

Goal

Join everything into one body and check, by numbers, that a printer can make it.

Every convex piece is turned into its hull, and all the hulls, the core behind the wall and the ground plate are united in one operation (Manifold). Then the gate reads the result:

  • watertight: the status of the manifold must be NoError;
  • one body: only one piece with any volume (a stone that touches the rest only along an edge would be a loose body);
  • overhang: the area of down-facing faces leaning more than 65 degrees from vertical must be at most 0.1% of the downward area, and the area between 55 and 65 degrees at most another 0.1%. The bed on the ground is not counted.

Nothing is culled and no threshold is ever changed to pass: a wall that fails is shown as failing. The line a printer lays down (0.45 mm) and its layer (0.2 mm) are also measured and shown, but not part of the verdict.

Takes in
all the pieces: stones, core, ground
Hands on
one body and a verdict: pass, or which check failed
