Step 10 (L5) The union and the gate

The pieces are joined into one body, and the gate checks that it can print.

Every convex piece is turned into its hull, and the hulls, the core behind the wall and the ground plate are united in one operation (Manifold). Then the gate reads the result. Watertight: the manifold’s status must be NoError. One body: only one piece with any volume. Overhang: the area of down-facing faces leaning more than {{STEEP}} degrees must be at most a thousandth of the downward area, and the area between {{OVER}} and {{STEEP}} degrees at most another thousandth; the bed on the ground is not counted. The editor also shows the printer’s line ({{LINE}} mm) and layer ({{LAYER}} mm) in the Lines view and which faces are bridges; these are shown beside the verdict, not part of it.

Why. Nothing is culled and no threshold is ever changed to pass: a wall that fails is shown as failing.
