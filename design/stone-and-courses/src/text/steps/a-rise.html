Step 4 (L2) Rise: a stone carries on

A stone rolls, again and again, to carry on up through the next row.

Every stone starts in one row. It rolls against rise: on success it carries on into the next row and rolls again, stopping at the first failure or at the top. At 0 every stone is one row; near 1 stones run all the way up. A stone that rises keeps its footprint in the rows above (step 3 cuts the other cells round it) and its top is the top plane of the last row it reaches. A stone on a lip row never rises.

Why. A tall stone is one convex piece, so it is never a bridge; the stones above it rest on the stones below as before.
