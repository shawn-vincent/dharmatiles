Step 1 (L0) The footprint and the crest

The wall stands inside a box: the footprint, kept {{EDGE_MARGIN}} mm inside the tile edge, and the crest above it.

The tile is a {{SQUARE}} mm square (DungeonBlocks) or {{SQUARE_OL}} mm (OpenLOCK); a wall of Run 2x1 is two squares long. The footprint is the rectangle left after the margin, from the preset’s front back by region depth; a preset with a wing makes it an L, so a corner can be built. The crest is the height of the top along the wall. It is drawn by the graded rule: a smooth wobble with crest bumps per square, ranked, and each rank mapped through the crest weighting into the band between the two ends of top, so the crest uses its whole band at every seed. The dirt is {{DIRT}} mm thick and the crest is measured above it.

Why. Everything later is measured against this box, and the margin is what lets two tiles stand side by side.
