The wall is built in thirteen steps, in four parts that are the editor’s four panels: the region, the courses that fill it, each stone’s own region, and the rock in it. Each step says what it does in one sentence, shows the photograph it aims at, explains how it works, draws the mechanism, and lists the controls that drive it.
