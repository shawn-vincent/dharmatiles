The steps' own times and triangles are in each step above; the Costs view in the editor shows them for the wall on screen. Today’s Rust engine makes 0.54 to 3.2 million triangles for a cliff tile (library baseline, 15 Nov), mostly grain, which this model does not make.

Where the triangles are

Preset L, 1×1: of the 98984 triangles the union keeps, 16794 face up, 60807 face sideways and 8719 face down (the ground bed is not counted). More, smaller stones cost more: R (472 solids) takes 1659 ms against 822 ms for L; T and S, with fewer, taller or wider stones, take about half.
