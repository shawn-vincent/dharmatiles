Blocky Cliff Generator

One formation from one seed, piled from rounded boxes and built as one solid: the stones are joined by a plain union, so every inside corner is sharp, and each joint that shows is a V groove of 0.05 figure heights or less with flat flanks, closing up (fusing) along part of its length by the measured rule of chapter 8. The rules are those of the Blocky Cliff Atlas. It is for judging the coarse character: shapes, sizes, arrangement, proportion. No figure, no base, no surface.
