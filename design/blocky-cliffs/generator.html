Blocky Cliff Generator

One formation from one seed, piled from plain chamfered boxes by the rules of the Blocky Cliff Atlas. It is for judging the coarse character: shapes, sizes, arrangement, proportion. No figure, no base, no surface.

Seed

Drag to turn. Pinch to zoom. Scrolling over the view scrolls the page. Lengths are in figure heights: a formation is about two high.

Seeds from the one above onward, each drawn once as a still picture from the references' three-quarter view. Choose one to load it.

What this does not do: the stones are flat-faced boxes where the references are softly rounded; edges are ruled where the references' wander; dipping beds are stiff; and two forms that each appear once in the sheets, a niche with a chockstone (J) and a rock shelter (K), are not made at all.
