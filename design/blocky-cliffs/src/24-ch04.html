Chapter 4

Catalogue of stones

One primitive, six roles. A role is where a stone stands and what proportions it takes there, not a shape of its own: the scatter in chapter 5 shows the roles running into one another.

Measured "As seen" is face height ÷ width from the traces, 10th to 90th percentile, with the number of stones. Proposed Sizes in fh are true sizes for a generator, set so that stones drawn from them measure like the references when seen from the references' camera (chapter 14 checks this, and checks the relations between stones too). Estimated Roles were given by eye while tracing; the traced examples are outlined in blue.
