Blocky Cliff Atlas

A geometric study of four reference sheets of stylised cliffs built from large, smooth, block-like stones: what the stones are, how they sit against each other, what varies and what never does, and the smallest set of rules that would make more of them.

What each colour marks a stone's outline top faces, ledges, levels joints, clefts, the space between axes: lean, dip, direction dimensions and scale
Names A–P: the sixteen labelled forms (two sheets show the same sixteen). T1–T20: the sheet of twenty, row by row. S1–S16: the sheet of sixteen, row by row. 52 formations in all. Forms are drawn plain grey; nothing on a form is red or orange.
