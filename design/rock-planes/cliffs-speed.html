Cliffs: speed ideas

Every idea for making a Cliffs from Spines wall build faster, so none is lost: what it is, why it works, what it saves and where it stands. The aim is under 1 s for a wall, under 0.5 s if possible. Times are Node, seed 1; m is measured, e is estimated. As of 23 Nov 2026: Fieldstone builds in 1.87 s (masses 0.62 + rocks 1.26), Layered 1.70, Ashlar 0.67, Granite 0.53, Columns 0.09.

Where the time goes

A stone's bottom rests on the finished stones under it, so stones are built in a chain: 49 levels in Fieldstone (about 18 stones per level), 56 in Layered (about 7). The cost is spread over every stage; no single kernel dominates (clip is about 20%). Fieldstone, per seed: carving the stones (stoneOf) 555 ms, the mass split 420, paint 195, holding 127, masses 95, bottoms 81, rest grid 67.

Carving cost (24 Nov)

Carving cuts by edges (bfbcd945): each seen edge is bevelled once, or twice when Tilt reaches 12°, in place of area-spread facets, chamfers and rings. Fieldstone per build: 151 ms in Node, 177 ms in Chrome (carving 80 ms), 46 planes a stone, 90k triangles; one cut per edge would be 138 ms in Chrome (carving 47). Of the 46 planes about 20 are contacts: crowns, crown domes and the stepped bottom. Crown planning in holding is about 39 ms (chains 11, the two-holder search 9). Fuse 40 builds take about 2.4 s. Measured and not worth it: template plane sets (plane choice is 0.7 µs a plane; the cost is the count, not retries), one halfspace intersection instead of clips (0.64 µs a clip, 69% of clips cut), bounding-sphere early outs (about 3 ms at best). Next: the stepped bottom folded into the main flare, crown planning without the chain search, Fuse 40's cost.
