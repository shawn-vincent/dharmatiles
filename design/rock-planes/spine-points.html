Column dots only

The column is a dot at each level, straight up from the base dot (or leaning). The rest of the cloud is ordinary darts kept a spacing from every dot. Each level's cell is shaped by whatever dots happen to sit near it, so the slices differ and the column is ragged: steps where one level's cell sticks out past the next.

Neighbours cloned up

The base dot's cell is found in the cloud first. The dots that make its sides (those within half a level of its height) are copied up with the column, each moved by the same step as the column's dots. Then the rest of the cloud is placed around them, kept out of the balls through the column cells' corners. A neighbour that sits a little above or below the base makes a sloped wall, and a sloped wall copied up makes a sawtooth.

Mirrored, straight walls

The base dot's cell is cut at its own height, giving the column's outline P. For each edge of P, each column dot is mirrored across that edge's wall (upright, or along the lean), and one dot below and one above the column mirror it across its end faces. The column's cells then stack into one prism exactly, as long as the rest of the cloud keeps out of the balls through their corners.

Spines, a plane per pair

ChatGPT's rule, with no dots at all. Each stone is a segment, its spine. It is cut off flat at both of its spine's ends, and for every other spine it is cut by the plane halfway between the two spines' closest points. Every stone comes out convex and keeps its whole spine, but the planes are chosen pair by pair and don't agree with each other, and nothing grows past a spine's end, so some space belongs to no stone. That space is drawn in blue. The column is the teal spine, from the bottom of the lowest level to the top of the highest. Rows run along the wall; full columns run floor to top; stacked columns are shorter columns one above another with the end gap between them.
