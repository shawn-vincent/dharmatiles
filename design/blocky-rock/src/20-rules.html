2 · The rules

One idea: chamfer every edge

Round 6 strips the rock to one idea. Every number is proposed. Lengths are in figure heights (fh); m is the shorter plan side.

B1 Base: a box, or a ramp

Class shares of the sheet: slab .22, block .30, pillar .22, ramp .26. Plan W, D and height H: slab W 0.75–1.1, D 0.6–0.76 W, H 0.28–0.36 W; block W 0.5–0.75, D 0.75–0.95 W, H 0.68–0.95 W; pillar W 0.42–0.56, D 0.7–0.9 W, H 1.7–2.1 W (not above 1.3 fh); ramp W 0.5–0.7, D 0.65–0.85, H 0.62–0.82, rising toward the back.

B2 Nothing is square

Each outer side leans by its own −0.03 to 0.07 and is twisted in plan by up to 0.07; the top slopes up to 0.045 each way. The bottom is flat.

C1 Every edge is a wandering strip

24 segments an edge. The width is a smooth random curve (two sine waves) around a base width, varying ±38 per cent, and the tilt is another, so the strip swells, narrows and twists. A slow bow of up to 0.3 of the width, in or out, runs along it.

C2 Angles by kind of edge

  • top edge: base width 0.07–0.13 m, 8–27 degrees below horizontal (twist ±5), a short drop down the side;
  • bottom edge: width 0.04–0.07 m, 60–69 degrees above horizontal (twist ±4);
  • side edge: width 0.05–0.10 m, 18–72 degrees (twist ±10): into either face.

On one face the chamfers together never take more than a third of its width (each leg at most 0.16 of the face's width).

F1 Faces curve a little

Each face (not the bottom) carries a dome or saddle with an amplitude of 0.4–1.4 per cent of its size and a small tilt term, each from slightly concave to slightly convex. The chamfer lines sit on the curved faces.

M1 Corners and closure

A strip ends short of the corner by about 0.9 of its neighbours' legs; a patch joins the six end points, its centre bulged 0.6 of the way toward the old corner. Faces are rings of quads and a fan; every vertex is shared, so the mesh is closed.

P1 Printing: off

The 55 degree rule is not enforced this round; the steepest underside is reported. bottomBevel: false still leaves the foot sharp, for the cliff.

X1 Off, kept in the code

Lobes and steps, cracks (a wandering faceted cut with the far piece moved aside), battered corners and edges, and kink planes: makeRock(seed, { full: true }).
