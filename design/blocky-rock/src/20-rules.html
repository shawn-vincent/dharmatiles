2 · The rules

One idea: chamfer every edge

Round 7 keeps the one idea of round 6, with single-arc chamfers. It strips the rock to one idea. Every number is proposed. Lengths are in figure heights (fh); m is the shorter plan side.

B1 Base: a box, or a ramp

Class shares of the sheet: slab .22, block .30, pillar .22, ramp .26. Plan W, D and height H: slab W 0.75–1.1, D 0.6–0.76 W, H 0.28–0.36 W; block W 0.5–0.75, D 0.75–0.95 W, H 0.68–0.95 W; pillar W 0.42–0.56, D 0.7–0.9 W, H 1.7–2.1 W (not above 1.3 fh); ramp W 0.5–0.7, D 0.65–0.85, H 0.62–0.82, rising toward the back.

B2 Nothing is square

Each outer side leans by its own −0.03 to 0.07 and is twisted in plan by up to 0.07; the top slopes up to 0.045 each way. The bottom is flat.

C1 Every edge is one arc

24 segments an edge. The width is wider or narrower in the middle, by up to 15 per cent either way; the tilt is constant or changes once, by up to ±4 degrees on a top edge, ±3 on a bottom edge, ±8 on a side edge; the strip bows by up to 0.15 of its width, in or out. Each edge has its own random numbers.

C2 Angles and widths

  • top edge: width 0.13–0.24 m (at least 0.06 fh), 8–27 degrees below horizontal, a short drop down the side;
  • bottom edge: width 0.14–0.22 m (at least 0.06), 37–41 degrees above horizontal (twist ±1.5), so it tucks under: the rise up the face is its width times tan 37–41;
  • side edge: width 0.12–0.22 m (at least 0.06), 32–58 degrees, so no dark gash

On one face the chamfers together never take more than a third of its width (each leg at most 0.16 of the face and 0.24 of the rock's thinnest side, so a slab never pinches). Each chamfer is shown as its own band: normals are not smoothed across bends over 6 degrees.

F1 Faces curve a little

Each face (not the bottom) carries a dome or saddle with an amplitude of 0.04–0.16 per cent of its size and a small tilt term, each from slightly concave to slightly convex. The chamfer lines sit on the curved faces.

M1 Corners and closure

The corner facet is one triangle whose vertices are, on each of the three faces, the point where its two chamfer lines meet. Every strip, face grid and facet shares those vertices, so the mesh is closed.

P1 Printing: bottom chamfers

A bottom chamfer is held at 35 degrees or more above horizontal (at most 55 from vertical), so it prints; the steepest underside is reported from the real mesh. The rest is not enforced. bottomBevel: false still leaves the foot sharp, for the cliff.

X1 Off, kept in the code

Lobes and steps, cracks (a wandering faceted cut with the far piece moved aside), battered corners and edges, and kink planes: makeRock(seed, { full: true }).
