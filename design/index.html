Design explorations

Saved copies of the look pages: interactive browser prototypes that work out how stone, walls and grass are made before the Rust elements are built. Each page runs on its own; open it in a browser. These are working pages, not the site: they are not served by GitHub Pages, and each one shows its design as it stood when it was saved (17 Nov 2026).

  • Weathered Stone
    One stone, broken rather than textured, in six stages (outline cuts, strata chunks, set-back and fall, chips, pockets, grain, wear). Its code is the spec for the Rust rock element.
  • Rock at Every Scale
    What makes a model read as rock, from all the cliff references and sheets: the scale hierarchy on the unpainted panels, each primitive (facets, edges, steps, chips, dishes, pits, ridges, cracks, undulation, grain) as a 3D model beside its sheet panel, how rock breaks (plane families, persistence, a skin over a core, shingles, stacked courses), the lessons of the Wall N and O rounds, and a recipe for building a wall. build.py builds the page from src/ and img/.
  • Cliff Cleaving
    A proposal for the cliff's words, read off the fifteen reference pictures: every wall is one volume cut by families of planes, each block at its own depth, the crest a height per block, faces hewn; with the language check and Orin's rulings. src/gen.py writes the page.
  • Wall O, Anew and Wall N, Anew
    Both walls rebuilt from scratch from Rock at Every Scale alone: the recipe as written, then two passes by eye, with a table of every rule kept or changed. Neither beats the iterated versions; Rock at Every Scale, section 8, says what that taught. src/build.py builds each page (--local needs a three.js r128 copy beside it).
  • Wall N
    A from-scratch generator aimed at a sloped reference (N: a ramp of stacked, chipped slabs on a dip slope), beside the picture at its camera; it shows a sloped wall is one cleave set parallel to the face, each slab ending at its own top. src/build.py builds the page.
  • Wall O
    A from-scratch generator aimed at one reference picture (O: stacked blocks at stepped depths, hewn faces), with no controls, beside the picture at its camera; its notes say which steps carried the look. src/build.py builds the page.
  • Stone and Courses
    Walls and cliffs as two pairs: the region and the courses that fill it, then each stone's region and the rock in it, with a live editor. Its rejected explorations are in decisions; src/ builds the page (with the rock library in rock/src/, shared with Weathered Stone).
  • Leaf and Scatter
    Grass as a language: a leaf, a cluster, a scatter and near, with the measured blade and tuft and explorers for the outline and the section.
  • Soft Areas
    How top= bands, clouds and value ranges work today, and areas on the same model: two outlines, an inner inside an outer, plain or with odds, and what a scatter, a cliff's sites and a rock's outline do inside the band. A discussion; nothing built.
  • The Plate and the Meadow
    A visual review of the site and editor (type, colour, layout, the 3D view). Marked deprecated on the page; kept for the record.
